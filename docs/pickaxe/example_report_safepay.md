## 2. SHOULD YOU BUILD THIS?
**Why it could work**
1. Freelancers whose yearly tax is ₹10,000 or more must pay advance tax in instalments, and shortfalls attract interest [3][4].
2. India's gig and platform workforce reached 1.2 crore in FY25, up from 77 lakh in FY21 [1].
3. Automated advance tax reserve transfers create recurring lock in that spreadsheets cannot match.

**Why it could fail**
1. Zoho Books is free for revenue up to ₹25 lakh [5], and ClearTax already owns tax filing trust and distribution.
2. Freelancers hesitate to share bank data with a new app, even through RBI licensed Account Aggregators [6].
3. Indian freelancers are price sensitive; without CA or platform partners, acquisition cost is unproven against a ₹299/month plan.

**Premortem**
*Imagine it is 18 months from now and the venture has not worked out. These are the three most likely reasons, each with its prevention.*

**Story 1: The trust wall**
A launch in freelancer communities drew plenty of signups, but most people stopped at the bank consent screen, and few of the rest came back after the first month.
**Prevent:** start with uploaded bank statements and ask for Account Aggregator consent after the first useful number.

**Story 2: The CAC spiral**
Paid ads cost more per customer than a ₹299/month plan earned back before people cancelled, and the runway ran out.
**Prevent:** no paid ads until a CA or platform channel brings in customers at a cost the plan can pay back.

**Story 3: Cloned for free**
Zoho Books added a free 'safe to spend' view for its small business users. Growth stalled overnight.
**Prevent:** own the automated advance tax reserve and the CA workflow, which a dashboard view cannot copy quickly.
