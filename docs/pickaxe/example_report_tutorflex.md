## 2. SHOULD YOU BUILD THIS?
**Why it could work**
1. Board year parents already hire 1:1 tutors under a deadline, so the pain is recurring and paid.
2. 24.8 crore students were enrolled in Indian schools in 2023-24 [1], so the reachable pool is large.
3. Session escrow and verified quality create lock in that a classifieds listing cannot copy quickly.

**Why it could fail**
1. UrbanPro already owns tutor discovery and reviews for Indian parents [2], and Superprof is a funded global alternative [3].
2. Trust is the constraint: a new brand asking parents to pay a stranger to enter the home or teach a child online.
3. Direct paid ads to acquire both parents and tutors are unproven against a 10% to 15% take rate.

**Premortem**
*Imagine it is 18 months from now and the venture has not worked out. These are the three most likely reasons, each with its prevention.*

**Story 1: The empty pin code**
Parents signed up in three metros, found two tutors, waited, then went back to UrbanPro and the neighbourhood coaching centre.
**Prevent:** launch in one pin code, with a concierge roster of 20 verified tutors, before any citywide ads.

**Story 2: The CAC spiral**
Paid ads to parents and tutors cost more than the take rate earned back, and the runway ran out before density appeared.
**Prevent:** no paid ads until a PTA or school channel brings in parents at a cost the take rate can pay back.

**Story 3: Listed for free**
UrbanPro added verified badges and in app payments. Growth stalled overnight.
**Prevent:** own the board year session contract and PTA distribution, which a listing badge cannot copy quickly.
